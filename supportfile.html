Stat! Support

Questions about gameplay, subscriptions, purchases, or ads? Find answers below, or reach out directly.

Need a hand with Stat!?

Email us at stat@blottergames.com about anything: a puzzle that looks wrong, a purchase, an ad, or an idea for the game. We read every message. Many answers are also in the FAQ and the how-to-play guides.

Contact Support

Q1I think a puzzle has a mistake

Please tell us. Email the date, the game and what looks wrong; a screenshot helps. We check every report against the puzzle library.

Q2My streak or statistics disappeared

Stat! has no accounts, so your progress is stored where you play. On the website it lives in your browser on that device, so clearing site data, using a private window or switching browsers starts afresh. In the iPhone and iPad app it is stored on the device. Progress on the website and in the app is kept separately.

Q3Restoring Stat! Pro on another device

Pro belongs to your Apple ID. On the other iPhone or iPad, signed in with the same Apple ID, open Stat!'s settings and tap Restore purchases.

Q4Managing or cancelling Stat! Pro

Apple bills and manages the subscription. On your iPhone or iPad, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Stat!. You can change or cancel there at any time, and the daily puzzles stay free. Apple handles refund requests at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Q5Daily reminders

In the app, turn on Daily reminder in Stat!'s settings for a 9:00 AM nudge that helps keep your streak alive.

Q6Ads on the website

Ads keep the daily puzzles free in the browser. Google, our advertising partner, may use cookies to choose ads, and you can turn off personalized ads in Google's Ads Settings; our privacy policy has the details. If an ad looks wrong or offensive, email us the page and a description so we can look into it.

Q7Playing in the browser

Stat! plays in any up-to-date browser at playstat.blottergames.com, on a phone, tablet or computer, with JavaScript turned on.

Q8Terms, privacy and medical advice

The iPhone and iPad app is licensed under Apple's standard end user license agreement, and our privacy policy covers the app and both websites. Stat! is for education only and is not medical advice; if you have a health concern, talk to a qualified clinician.
