Contact Support

Q1What is Stat!?

Stat! is a medical education game designed to make review more engaging through quick diagnostic mini-games. It is not medical advice. Please contact your healthcare provider for any concerns.

Q2Purchases & subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are processed through Apple. For billing issues, please contact Apple Support or email us with your question.

Q3Ads

Stat! may show ads to support the app. If you experience an issue with an ad, please contact us with details so we can look into it.

Q4Gameplay help

If something isn't working correctly or you have feedback about a mini-game, send us a message at stat@blottergames.com.
