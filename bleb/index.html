Learn Step 1 one small win at a time.

bleb is a game-style iPhone app that teaches the USMLE Step 1 curriculum from zero: short lessons, mini-games, spaced review, streaks, leagues and friends. Five minutes on the train counts.

Help and contact

Short lessons

Bite-sized lessons on a course path, written from scratch for bleb, that build Step 1 knowledge without the wall of text.

Games and review

Mini-games and spaced review bring each idea back in a new format, right when it is about to fade.

Friends and leagues

Cheer on friends with preset reactions, climb a weekly league and rep your school. Everything social has privacy controls, block and report.

Your privacy

Sign in with Apple (or Google where offered), no email address and no password. Delete your account in the app any time. Read the privacy policy.

bleb is an educational study tool for people aged 16 and over. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the USMLE program, FSMB, NBME or any publisher. Contact: bleb@blottergames.com
