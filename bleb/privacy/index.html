Privacy policy

Effective September 29, 2026. Last updated October 3, 2026.

This policy explains what the bleb iPhone app and this website ("bleb", "we", "us") collect, why, who can see it and what your choices are. It is written to be read. If anything is unclear, write to bleb@blottergames.com.

The short version.

  • You sign in with Apple, or with Google where the app offers it. Signing in never asks for, receives or stores your email address, and there is no password. The only email address bleb asks for is a school email, and only if you apply for the student price.
  • You do not need an account to study. Without one, your study history stays on your phone.
  • With an account, we store your study activity, your profile and your friends so leagues, streaks and leaderboards work, and cloud save uploads your study progress (your answers and mastery) so you can restore it on a new phone.
  • Your progress belongs to your account. Signing out saves it to your account first, then removes it from the phone; signing in again brings it back. Deleting your account also removes it from the phone.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • The free plan shows ads from Google AdMob, with or without an account. Some paid plans show fewer ads or none, as described in the app. Tracking for ads only happens if you allow it.
  • You can delete your account in the app (Settings, then Delete account). Everything is removed right away. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription: see Support for how to cancel.
  • bleb is for people aged 16 and over.

1. Who is responsible

bleb is the operator of the app and of this website, and the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: bleb@blottergames.com.

2. What we collect and why

Your account (Sign in with Apple)

bleb accounts are optional. You create one with Sign in with Apple or, where the app offers it, Sign in with Google (next section). With Apple, Apple gives us a unique identifier for you in bleb (the Apple user ID). If you choose to share your name when you sign in, Apple gives it to us once and we use it to make a short display name such as "Ana R.". We do not request your email address and we do not store one (the one exception is the school email you send if you apply for the student price, described below). We also keep an Apple authorization token, encrypted, for one purpose: to disconnect bleb from your Apple ID when you delete your account.

Sign in with Google (where the app offers it)

If you sign in with Google instead, Google gives us a unique identifier for you in bleb (the Google account ID) and your name, which we use to make a short display name. We ask Google only for your basic profile, never for your email address, and we do not keep any Google token. Signing in with Apple and with Google makes two separate bleb accounts.

Your profile and settings

A username (the one you choose when you join; it cannot be changed later, except that a name we generated for you can be replaced once), a display name, your avatar, your school if you pick one, your privacy settings (hide real name, share progress, take part in school competitions, be discoverable in search) and your notification preferences.

Learning activity

You can study without an account, and then nothing about your study history leaves your phone. When you are signed in, the app sends activity events: when they happened, what kind of activity it was, the XP earned, the number of lessons completed and the number of questions answered and answered correctly, plus boss points. We keep your streak, level and achievements. If you turn on progress sharing, your course progress, mastery count and accuracy are shown to other people; otherwise they stay private to you. We use this to run XP, streaks, weekly leagues, leaderboards and school competitions.

Cloud save

When you are signed in, cloud save backs up your study progress to our server so you can restore it on a new phone. It includes your attempts and mastery (which questions you answered, how you did and how well you know each topic), your review schedule, your daily and weekly activity, your coins and hearts, your missions and your achievements. The app sends it as one compressed file, together with a device identifier the app generates and a format version. The server stores the file as it is and does not read what is inside. Only you can download it. Without an account nothing is uploaded, and deleting your account deletes your cloud save. When you sign out, the app saves your progress to cloud save and then removes it from the phone, so another account signing in on the same phone starts from its own progress.

Student price (only if you apply)

The student price of the top plan is for students we have verified. If you apply for it in the app, you send us your full name, your phone number, your school email address, the name of your school and your year of study. A person on our team reads them to decide whether to approve the request; we do not send anything to that address or call that number, and we do not use them for anything else. Once we have decided, we delete your phone number and school email address; we keep your name, school, year and the decision with your account so we know your student price was approved. Only our team can see your request. Deleting your account deletes it.

Friends, reactions, blocks and reports

Friend requests and friendships, and the reactions you send and receive. Reactions are presets only (for example a clap or a flame): there is no free-text chat. If you block someone we store that block. If you report someone we store the report (the reason you picked and an optional note of up to 500 characters) so we can review it. We review reports within 24 hours.

Push notifications

If you allow notifications, we store your device's push token, whether it is a production or a test build, the app version, your time zone and your language and region. We use the time zone so we do not notify you between 10 pm and 8 am your time, and we use the token to send you notifications about friend requests, reactions, your league, school competitions and school achievements. You can switch each type off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Purchases

If you buy a subscription or in-app item, Apple tells us what was bought (the product, transaction identifiers, dates and whether a subscription is active, renewing, in a grace period, expired or refunded). We use it to know which plan you have. Apple handles payment: we never see or store your payment card or Apple ID payment details.

Technical logs

Our servers write technical logs of requests: the time, the address (path) requested, the response status, how long it took and a request ID. These logs do not contain request bodies, passwords or sign-in tokens. Our hosting provider also processes network-level information such as IP addresses to deliver traffic and keep the service secure.

Ads

The free plan shows ads served by Google AdMob, whether or not you have an account. Some paid plans show fewer ads and others show none, as described in the app. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect device and usage information such as your IP address and device identifiers. We only access the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and you can change that at any time in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form and only asks AdMob to use personal data for ads if you agree. Otherwise ads are non-personalized. Google's own description is at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

3. What other people can see

Other learners can see your username, avatar, level, XP, streak, league, achievements and school. They see your display name unless you switch on "hide real name". They see your progress only if you switch on progress sharing. If you turn off "discoverable" you do not appear in username search (people who already know your exact username can still send a request). If you turn off school competitions your activity is left out of school-versus-school results. Someone you block cannot find you, send you requests or reactions, or see you in shared lists.

4. Why we use your information

  • To provide the app: accounts, activity, leagues, friends, school groups, cloud save, purchases and notifications.
  • To keep bleb safe: reviewing reports, enforcing our terms, preventing cheating and abuse.
  • To show ads on the free plan (and fewer ads on some paid plans) and keep the app free.
  • To answer your messages and fix problems.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, our legal bases are: performing our contract with you (running your account and the features you use), your consent (notifications, ad tracking and personalized ads, which you can withdraw at any time), and our legitimate interests in security, preventing abuse and keeping the service working.

5. Who we share it with

  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases and push notifications.
  • Google AdMob: ads, as described above.
  • Google: Sign in with Google, only if you use it.
  • Railway: our hosting provider, which runs our servers and database in the United States.
  • Authorities and others when the law requires it, or to protect people's safety or our rights.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use it for anything except what is described here.

6. Where your data is stored

Our servers and database are hosted by Railway in the United States. If you use bleb from outside the United States, your data is transferred to and stored in the United States.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account data (profile, activity, friends, cloud save, devices, purchase records, blocks and reports) is kept until you delete your account.
  • Deleting your account removes all of it from our database immediately, including your cloud save, and we ask Apple to revoke the token that connects bleb to your Apple ID. If Apple cannot be reached at that moment we keep the encrypted token, and nothing else, for up to three days only to retry disconnecting it. Copies in backups go when those backups are deleted (see Backups below).
  • Backups. To recover from a failure we keep backups of our database: the automatic backups of our hosting provider and, at times, an encrypted copy we make ourselves. Every backup is deleted within 30 days. A deleted account remains only in backups made before it was deleted, until they are deleted, and is never used from there. If we ever have to restore a backup, every account deleted since that backup was made is deleted again before bleb is back online. For that we keep a one-way fingerprint (a hash) of each deleted account's internal identifier with the date of the deletion, and nothing else.
  • Suspended accounts. If we suspended your account for breaking our rules, you can still delete it and everything is removed as described, except that we keep a one-way fingerprint (a hash) of your Apple or Google sign-in identifier together with your username and the date. We keep it so a suspension cannot be dodged by creating a new account, and only for that purpose. Write to us if you think the suspension was a mistake.
  • Student price requests: the phone number and school email are deleted as soon as we decide the request; the rest is kept until you delete your account.
  • Server logs are kept only for a short time.
  • Notification records are cleared automatically after about a month, and App Store notification records after about three months.

8. How to delete your account

In the app, open Settings and choose Delete account. If you cannot use the app, email bleb@blottergames.com from any address, tell us your username and we will help; we may need to confirm the account is yours. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription: cancel that in iOS Settings (see Support).

9. Your rights

Wherever you live you can ask us to access, correct, delete or export (portability) the personal data we hold about you, and to stop using it for something you object to. You can correct most of it yourself in the app (display name, avatar, school, privacy and notification settings) and delete everything in Settings. For anything else, email bleb@blottergames.com. We answer within one month, or sooner where the law says so.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting what happened before. You can complain to your local data protection authority.

If you are in California

You have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it (this policy), to request access, correction and deletion, and not to be treated worse for using these rights. We do not sell personal information. If you consider personalized advertising a "sale" or "sharing", you can turn it off by declining tracking in iOS Settings, or by writing to us.

10. Security

Connections to bleb use HTTPS. We store sign-in tokens only as one-way hashes and the Apple authorization token encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, so we keep what we collect to a minimum.

11. Children

bleb is made for medical students and is not directed to children. You must be at least 16 years old to use it. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe someone under 16 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for changes that matter, tell you in the app or on this page before they take effect.
