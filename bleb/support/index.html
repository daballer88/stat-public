Common questions

How do I sign in?

bleb uses Sign in with Apple (and Sign in with Google, where the app offers it): tap the button in the app. There is no password and we never see your email address. If it will not work, check that you are signed in to your Apple ID on the phone, update iOS and try again. Signing in with a different Apple ID opens a different bleb account.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open bleb, go to Settings and choose Restore purchases. You do not need a bleb account, but the iPhone must be signed in to the same Apple ID you bought with (iOS Settings, your name). Your plan comes back within a moment. If it does not, email us with the app version and the date of the purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose bleb and tap Cancel. Apple handles all subscriptions, so this is the only place to cancel, and deleting the app or your bleb account does not cancel it. You keep your plan until the end of the period you already paid for.

What happens when I sign out?

Your progress belongs to your bleb account. When you sign out, bleb first saves your latest progress to your account, then removes it from the phone and shows the welcome screen. Sign in again, on this phone or a new one, and it comes back. If bleb cannot save first (for example, you are offline), it asks before signing you out. Your App Store subscription stays with your Apple ID either way.

Lost a phone, or signed in somewhere you no longer use? Choose Sign out of all devices instead: bleb saves your progress, then signs your account out everywhere, this phone included. If you cannot open the app, email us your username and we will do it for you.

How do I delete my account?

In bleb open Settings and choose Delete account. Your account, activity, friends, cloud save and purchase records are removed right away, and the app clears your progress from the phone. Deleting your account does not cancel your App Store subscription. To cancel it, open iOS Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose bleb and tap Cancel Subscription (see above). If you cannot open the app, email us your username and we will help.

How do I report or block someone?

Open their profile and choose Block or Report. Pick a reason (name, avatar, harassment, cheating or other) and add a short note if it helps. Blocking is instant: you will not see each other in search, friend lists, leaderboards or leagues, and they cannot send you requests or reactions. We review every report within 24 hours and act on content and users that break our rules: we remove or reset names and avatars and suspend or remove accounts.

Why am I (not) getting notifications?

Check that notifications are allowed for bleb in iOS Settings, then check the switches for friends, reactions, leagues and schools in the app's notification settings. We stay quiet from 10 pm to 8 am your time and send at most three reaction notifications and five friend-request notifications a day. If you turned notifications on after installing, open the app once so your phone can register.

Can I use bleb offline?

Yes. Lessons and reviews you have on your phone work without a connection, and your progress is kept on the device and sent when you are back online. Leagues, friends, school boards, purchases and cloud save need a connection.

Why do I see ads?

The free plan shows ads, with or without a bleb account. Some paid plans show fewer ads and others show none: the plans and what they include are in the app (Settings, then Subscription). You can choose whether ads may be personalized in the consent form (where your region requires it) and in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.

How old do I have to be?

You must be at least 16 years old to use bleb. If you think someone under 16 has an account, email us and we will delete it.
