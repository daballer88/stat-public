Three ways to play

Three minigames, one sharper clinician

Each tests a different muscle of clinical reasoning — from building a differential to spotting the patterns that tie a disease together.

Diagnose

Syndrome

Uncover the diagnosis step by step. Guess vitals, symptoms, labs, and imaging to learn what's normal, abnormal, present, or absent — and narrow your differential to the final answer.

Strategize

Traits

Think strategically. Guess diagnoses and compare them across system, type, acuity, treatment, and more. Each attempt eliminates possibilities until you land on the correct answer.

Connect

Associations

Group related clinical findings into diseases. Inspired by connection-style puzzles, Associations challenges you to recognize which symptoms belong together — and the diseases behind them.
