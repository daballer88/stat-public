Stat! is a medical education game that provides daily diagnostic mini-games and related gameplay features. Here's what we collect and how it's used.

If Stat! includes online multiplayer or other online features, information necessary to provide those features may be processed to connect players and operate gameplay.

Stat! is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

/ 07Data Sharing

We may share data with service providers and third-party partners, such as Google AdMob, only as needed to operate, improve, and monetize the app.

/ 08Your Choices

You can manage app permissions through your device settings. You can also limit ad tracking or change tracking permissions through iOS privacy settings.
