Privacy Policy

Stat! is a medical education game that provides daily diagnostic minigames and related gameplay features, in the iPhone and iPad app and on our websites, blottergames.com and playstat.blottergames.com. Here's what we collect and how it's used.

Stat! — Medical Minigames · Effective May 13, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026

01Information We Collect

Stat! may collect limited information necessary to operate and improve the app and websites, including gameplay-related data, app usage information, device information, diagnostics, and advertising-related identifiers. On the websites, your game progress, statistics and settings are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device; Stat! has no accounts.

02Advertising in the App

Stat! uses Google AdMob to display advertisements in the app. Google AdMob may collect and use information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage data, diagnostics, and advertising data to provide, measure, and improve ads.

03Advertising and Cookies on Our Websites

Our websites use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our websites or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, and you can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

04Tracking and App Tracking Transparency

If Stat! requests permission to track, your permission may allow advertising partners to use identifiers such as the device advertising identifier to deliver personalized ads and measure ad performance. You can change tracking permissions at any time in your device settings.

05Stat! Pro and Purchases

Stat! offers an optional Pro subscription in the app. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store (or by Google Play on Android), and Stat! does not collect or store your payment card information.

06Online Games

Online games against a friend, part of Stat! Pro, connect the two players through our game server. While a game is open, the server holds a room code, the two connections and each player's IP address in memory, to pass moves between the players and to limit abuse. Nothing about the game is saved after the room closes, and a room nobody uses is dropped after an hour.

07Children's Privacy

Stat! is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

08Data Sharing

We may share data with service providers and third-party partners, such as Google AdMob and Google AdSense, only as needed to operate, improve, and monetize the app and websites.

09Hosting and Fonts

blottergames.com is hosted on GitHub Pages and playstat.blottergames.com on Railway. Like any web host, they process standard request data, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver pages. blottergames.com also loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers when one of its pages opens; see Google's privacy policy.

10Your Choices

You can manage app permissions through your device settings, and limit ad tracking or change tracking permissions through iOS privacy settings. On the websites, you can manage or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings, and opt out of personalized ads as described above.
