Stat! Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 13, 2026

Stat! is a medical education game app that provides daily diagnostic mini-games and related gameplay features.

Information We Collect

Stat! may collect limited information necessary to operate and improve the app, including gameplay-related data, app usage information, device information, diagnostics, and advertising-related identifiers.

Advertising

Stat! uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and use information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage data, diagnostics, and advertising data to provide, measure, and improve ads.

Tracking and App Tracking Transparency

If Stat! requests permission to track, your permission may allow advertising partners to use identifiers such as the device advertising identifier to deliver personalized ads and measure ad performance. You can change tracking permissions at any time in your device settings.

Pro Version and Purchases

Stat! may offer a Pro version or in-app purchases. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Stat! does not directly collect or store your payment card information.

Online Features

If Stat! includes online multiplayer or other online features, information necessary to provide those features may be processed to connect players and operate gameplay.

Children's Privacy

Stat! is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Sharing

We may share data with service providers and third-party partners, such as Google AdMob, only as needed to operate, improve, and monetize the app.

Your Choices

You can manage app permissions through your device settings. You can also limit ad tracking or change tracking permissions through iOS privacy settings.
